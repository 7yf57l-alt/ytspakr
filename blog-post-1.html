10 Viral Title Formulas That Get Millions of Views

March 15, 2026  |  8 min read  |  Advanced

Your video title is the FIRST thing viewers see. It determines whether they click or scroll past. After analyzing millions of viral videos, here are the 10 proven title formulas that consistently get millions of views.

1. The "Number + Result" Formula

"5 Ways to [Achieve Result]"

Examples: "5 Ways to Get 1000 Subscribers Fast", "7 Editing Tricks That Made My Videos Go Viral"

Why it works: Numbers promise specific, actionable value. Results create desire.

2. The Curiosity Gap

"I Tried [Something] for [Time] — Here's What Happened"

Examples: "I Posted Daily for 30 Days — Here's What Happened", "I Tried Faceless YouTube for 1 Month"

Why it works: Creates mystery that viewers MUST resolve by clicking.

3. The Controversial Statement

"Why [Common Belief] Is WRONG"

Examples: "Why Subscriber Count Doesn't Matter", "Why You SHOULDN'T Post Daily"

Why it works: Challenges assumptions and sparks debate.

4. The Secret Revealed

"The Secret to [Desired Outcome] Nobody Tells You"

Examples: "The Secret to Getting 1M Views Nobody Tells You", "Hidden YouTube Algorithm Secrets"

Why it works: Promises exclusive, insider knowledge.

5. The "Stop Doing X" Formula

"Stop Doing [Bad Habit] Until You Watch This 🔥"

Examples: "Stop Posting at 3 PM Until You Watch This", "Stop Making Boring Thumbnails"

Why it works: Creates urgency and addresses pain points.

6. The "I Made $X" Formula

"How I Made $[Amount] Using [Method]"

Examples: "How I Made $10k Using YouTube Shorts", "How I Made $50k with 500 Subscribers"

Why it works: Money motivates. Specific numbers build credibility.

7. The Emotional Story

"How [Event] Changed My Life Forever (Emotional)"

Examples: "How Losing My Job Made Me a YouTuber", "The Video That Changed Everything"

Why it works: Emotional content drives higher engagement and shares.

8. The "X vs Y" Comparison

"[Option A] vs [Option B] — Which Is Better?"

Examples: "YouTube Shorts vs Long Form — Which Grows Faster?", "Adobe vs DaVinci Resolve"

Why it works: Comparison content is highly searchable and shareable.

9. The "Truth About" Formula

"The Ugly Truth About [Topic] Nobody Talks About"

Examples: "The Ugly Truth About Being a Full-Time YouTuber", "The Reality of Making Money Online"

Why it works: Promises honest, unfiltered information.

10. The "In 2026" Update Formula

"[Topic] in 2026: Is It Still Worth It?"

Examples: "Buying a YouTube Channel in 2026: Is It Still Worth It?", "YouTube Automation in 2026"

Why it works: Timely content ranks well and addresses current concerns.

🎯 Pro Tips for Title Success

  • Keep titles under 60 characters — longer titles get cut off on mobile
  • Use capital letters strategically — but avoid ALL CAPS
  • Add 1-2 emojis — they increase CTR by up to 15%
  • A/B test everything — try different titles for the same video
  • Match title to thumbnail — they should tell the same story
